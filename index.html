About Our School

A learning community built on trust, discipline and genuine care

Nightingale English Medium School was founded in 1998 by a group of educators who wanted to bring structured, English-medium education to families in Imphal. What started as a single building with six classrooms has grown into a full campus serving students from Nursery through Class 12.

We follow the CBSE curriculum and keep our class sizes manageable so that teachers can actually know the students they teach. Our approach balances academic rigour with sports, arts and the kind of everyday guidance that helps children grow into capable, considerate adults.

How We Teach

Our Teaching Methodology

We rely on methods that are proven to work, applied consistently rather than reinvented each term.

1

Activity-Based Learning

Concepts are taught through hands-on activities and real examples before moving to abstract problems.

2

Continuous Assessment

Regular class tests and assignments help us catch gaps early, instead of waiting for term exams.

3

Smart Classroom Integration

Select classrooms use digital boards to supplement — not replace — direct teaching.

4

Individual Attention

Teachers maintain progress notes for every student and flag concerns to parents early.

5

Doubt-Clearing Sessions

Weekly subject-wise sessions where students can ask questions outside regular class time.

6

Peer Learning Groups

Structured group work builds collaboration skills alongside academic understanding.

Frequently Asked Questions

A child must be at least 3 years old by 31st March of the academic session for admission to Nursery. We follow the age criteria set by the state education department.
For Nursery to Class 1, admission is based on age and seat availability. From Class 2 onward, a short written assessment and an interaction with parents is part of the process.
Yes, we operate GPS-tracked buses across 12 routes covering most parts of Imphal. Route details and charges are available from the front office.
English is the medium of instruction for all subjects, with Manipuri taught as a core subject to maintain connection with the regional language and culture.
Physical education is part of the regular timetable for all students. Club activities such as art, music and debate are optional but strongly encouraged.
Fees can be paid online through the parent portal, by bank transfer, or in person at the school's accounts office during working hours.
